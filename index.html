Seattle Resources

Shelter, housing, showers and crisis help. Closest options to King Street Station and Pioneer Square.

Crisis help — call or text 988 Free, 24 hours, any day. Someone will pick up. King County Crisis Line — 206-461-3222 24 hours. Can send a team to you in person.

A place to sleep tonight

Operation Nightwatch
302 14th Ave S
Open 8pm to 9am, every night. Walk in. They find you a downtown shelter bed.
Call Map
Best first stop at night. If it is late and nothing else is open, start here.
Union Gospel Mission — Men's Shelter
318 2nd Ave Ext S
Low barrier. Bed, case worker, basic needs. Longer stays up to 30 days. Christian organization.
Call Map
Bread of Life Mission
97 S Main St
For single men. Open 24 hours.
Call Map
Call before going. One source says $5 and a photo ID are required any time. Another says come 9:30–11am Tuesday to Friday with no ID needed. We could not confirm which is right.
Salvation Army — William Booth Center
811 Maynard Ave S
Short-term housing programs for men. Clean and sober only.

Getting into housing

Catholic Community Services
100 23rd Ave S
Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm. Appointment only — call first. This is a way into King County's housing list (Coordinated Entry).
Call Map
Solid Ground — North Seattle
Walk-in hours Wednesdays, 11am–1pm. No appointment needed during that window.
Call
Safe parking — Urban League
If you have a vehicle to sleep in. Lots open 7 days a week.
Call
Housing through Coordinated Entry takes time, and there are not enough places in King County for everyone waiting. Getting on the list early still matters — but keep using shelters meanwhile.

Also worth knowing

Seattle Central Library
1000 4th Ave
Free computers and internet. Somewhere warm to sit. Use it for email if you have no phone.
