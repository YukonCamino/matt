Seattle Resources

Near King Street Station and Pioneer Square.

Crisis help — call or text 988 Free, 24 hours, any day. King County Crisis Line — 206-461-3222 24 hours. Can send someone to you in person.
Call before you walk anywhere. Hours and rules change often, and some of what is below could not be confirmed. A phone call saves an hour of walking. On each place: green = confirmed · orange = could not confirm, ask on the phone · grey = not offered

Showers, laundry, help — best first stop

Compass Hygiene Center & Day Center
77 S Washington St — a few minutes from King Street Station
Showers & laundry: Mon–Fri 7am–4pm, Sat 8am–12pm
Day center: Mon–Fri 9am–4pm
  • Showers
  • Laundry
  • Housing help
  • Phone to use
  • No beds
  • Meals — ask
Free. Adults who are homeless.
Call hygiene Call day center Map
If you only go one place, make it this one. Showers, laundry and someone who can start housing paperwork, all in one building, close to where you are.
Urban Rest Stop — Downtown
1924 9th Ave
Arrive a weekday morning, around 8am. That time works under every schedule we found. Closed 12–1pm.
  • Showers — 1 a day
  • Laundry — 1 load every 2 days
  • Restrooms
  • No beds
  • No meals
Free. Soap and towels provided.
Call Map
Four different schedules are posted in different places, and weekend hours are the least reliable. Shower sign-up has to be done in person. Laundry takes about 2 hours.

A place to sleep

Operation Nightwatch
302 14th Ave S
Arrive 8pm. Hot meal at 8pm, then they find you a downtown shelter bed.
  • Meal at 8pm
  • Sends you to a bed
  • Housing help — ask
  • No showers
  • No laundry
Free. Single men 18+. No ID requirement mentioned.
Call Map
Best option late at night. They do not keep many beds here — what they do is get you into one somewhere downtown. Sources disagree on closing time, so do not arrive late.
Bread of Life Mission
97 S Main St — close to King Street Station
Shelter open 24 hours. To get a bed, sign up 5:30–6:15pm. Office Mon–Fri 9:30am–2pm.
  • Beds — men 18+
  • Showers
  • Laundry
  • Meals
  • Housing help — ask
Bring $5 and photo ID if you can — see below. Sleeping area is up 3 flights of stairs.
Call Map
The newest guide says $5 and valid ID required. County listings say there is no fee. Bring both if you have them. Guests are asked to commit to staying sober. Storage also available: $20/month plus a $15 key deposit you get back.

Replacing your ID, phone calls, mail

Solanus Casey Center
804 9th Ave, at St. James Cathedral
Mon–Thu, arrive between 1pm and 3pm. Closed Fri, Sat, Sun.
  • WA ID & licence help
  • Birth certificates
  • Phones for local calls
  • Computers
  • Socks, toiletries, ponchos
  • No showers
  • No beds
  • No meals
Free. Coffee, tea and snacks only — not a meal site.
Call Map
Go here to replace an ID. Several shelters want one. They also note that money is tight and they cannot promise every request. Bus passes are given out by lottery on the last Wednesday of the month.
Seattle Indian Center
1265 S Main St, Suite 105
Mon–Fri 9:30am–5:30pm
  • Showers
  • Laundry
  • Mailing address
  • No beds
Free, open to everyone. A current state ID is needed to use the mail service.
Call Map
A fixed mailing address is worth setting up early — benefits, court letters and job replies all need somewhere to go.

Getting into housing

Solid Ground — North Seattle
1501 N 45th St (Wallingford)
Walk in for a housing assessment Wednesday 11am–1pm, first come first served. Office hours differ by source — call.
  • Housing assessment
  • Tenant & eviction help
  • No beds here
  • No showers
  • No laundry
Free. This is North Seattle's official way onto the county housing list.
Call Map
There is a food pantry in the same building run by FamilyWorks: Tue 12–2pm, Thu 3–6pm, Fri 11am–1pm. Worth timing your visit around it.
Catholic Community Services
100 23rd Ave S
Appointment only, Mon–Fri 9am–5pm. Do not walk in.
  • Housing list access
  • No beds
  • No showers
  • No meals
Free. Phone first and book a time.
Call
Mostly offices, and walk-ins usually cannot be seen. If you want Catholic Community Services in person, go to the Solanus Casey Center above instead.

About job help: none of these places advertise job placement, so we have not listed it anywhere. The day centres — Compass especially — are the right people to ask, and they will know who in Seattle does it.

About ID: carry it if you have it. Only Bread of Life clearly asks for one, but it comes up often. The Solanus Casey Center replaces lost Washington ID.

Put together from the King County Crisis Connections shelter, food and services guides, the City of Seattle resource guide, and each organisation's own website. The county guides are a few months old, which is why calling first matters.
